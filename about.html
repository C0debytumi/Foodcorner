Our story

Started with one grill and a corner stand

Food Corner opened as a single grill on the pavement outside a hardware shop. Ten years on, we're still run by the same family, cooking the same way — fresh, unhurried, and made when you order it, not before.

We buy from growers and butchers we know by name, and we change the specials board around what's good that week rather than a fixed script.
